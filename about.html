Our story

A clinic built around trust

Vetriva Animal Hospital is a veterinary clinic in Islamabad. We combine modern veterinary medicine with a calm, welcoming environment, so visits are easier for pets and for the people who love them.

From a first vaccination to advanced diagnostics and surgery, our aim is simple: to look after every animal as if they were our own, and to give you the clear, honest information you need to make good decisions.

  • Dogs
  • Cats
  • Birds
  • Rabbits
  • Small pets

What we stand for

The values behind everything we do for you and your pet.

Compassion first

Every pet is treated like family. We take time to listen, and we handle every animal gently.

Expert care

Qualified veterinarians who explain every step and every option in plain words.

Honest advice

Clear, honest recommendations, with no pressure, so you can make the right choice for your pet.

Clean and calm

A clean, welcoming clinic with low-stress handling that helps anxious pets relax.
