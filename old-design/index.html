FAQ

Questions pet parents ask

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can give your pet our full attention, but walk-ins are welcome and emergencies are always prioritised.

Which animals do you treat?

We treat dogs, cats, birds, rabbits and other small companion animals. If you're unsure, just call and ask.

How often should my pet be vaccinated?

Puppies and kittens need a series of vaccines in their first months, followed by boosters. Adult pets usually need yearly boosters. Your vet will create a schedule for your pet.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Bring any previous medical or vaccination records, a list of current medicines and food, and keep your pet on a leash or in a secure carrier.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call us right away so we can prepare for your arrival and advise you on safe first steps while you travel.
