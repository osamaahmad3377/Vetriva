About this service

What is ultrasound?

Ultrasound uses sound waves to create live pictures of the organs, such as the liver, kidneys, bladder and womb. It is non-invasive and helps your vet find problems that X-rays can miss.

What is included

  • Abdominal ultrasound
  • Pregnancy checks
  • Examination of organs such as liver, kidneys, bladder and spleen
  • Guided sampling when needed
  • Explanation of the findings
Book now

When to book

Common reasons to come in for ultrasound.

Tummy or urinary problems

Vomiting, diarrhoea, weight loss or blood in urine.

Abnormal blood tests

To look at the organs more closely.

Pregnancy

To confirm and monitor a pregnancy.

How it works

Simple from start to finish.

  1. 1

    Preparation

    Your vet may ask you to withhold food for a few hours.

  2. 2

    Scan

    A small area of fur may be clipped. The scan is painless.

  3. 3

    Findings

    Your vet explains what they see and recommends next steps.

FAQ

Questions about ultrasound

Can’t find your answer? Get in touch and we’ll be happy to help.

Does it hurt?

No. Ultrasound is painless. Most pets lie calmly while the scan is done.

Will my pet’s fur be shaved?

A small patch is usually clipped so the images are clear. It grows back.

How should I prepare?

Your vet will tell you at booking whether to withhold food and whether your pet should have a full bladder.
